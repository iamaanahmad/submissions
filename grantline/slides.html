The problem and the agent

A grant match needs reasons.

InputOrganization region, focus, funding need, and grant records with source links.
DecisionCheck date, region, focus, amount, source, and duplicate identity. Rank the survivors.
OutputCandidate and exclusion cards show each rule and the linked source.
TraceExport every decision as JSONL for review and correction.
Missing evidence fails closed. A match is a lead, not proof of eligibility.
