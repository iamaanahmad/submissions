# Awaaz Kit · replacement demo guide

Record this on the device that runs the app. Keep the public YouTube video under three minutes. Use your natural voice and record the app itself. The [Kiroween rules](https://kiroween.devpost.com/rules) require a working-device demo and a public YouTube link.

## Suggested sequence

1. **0:00–0:15 · Set the problem.** Small shops need a fast way to turn a shopping request into an order or cash memo. Show both app links.
2. **0:15–1:15 · Order Awaaz.** Open [the order app](https://awaazkit.vercel.app/order/). Load the labelled demo shop if the catalog is empty. Type `do kilo chini aur teen kilo aloo`. Review the two generic items and confirm. Keep branded catalog items out of the recording.
3. **1:15–2:05 · Bill Awaaz.** Open [the bill app](https://awaazkit.vercel.app/bill/). Type `तीन किलो आलू और दो किलो प्याज और एक किलो टमाटर`, or choose its labelled sample. Review the lines and total before saving. Speak the amount shown by the app; do not rely on a scripted total.
4. **2:05–2:40 · Show reuse and Kiro.** Show the [two app folders](https://github.com/iamaanahmad/awaaz-kit/tree/main/apps), the [shared packages](https://github.com/iamaanahmad/awaaz-kit/tree/main/packages), and the [Kiro spec](https://github.com/iamaanahmad/awaaz-kit/tree/main/.kiro/specs/awaaz-kit). Explain one Kiro check that changed the build, such as the guard that blocks deleting a finalized bill item.

## Voice proof boundary

The typed flows above have been checked in the live apps. A model download alone does not prove spoken capture. If you record speech, show the real microphone input, the resulting transcript, and the review step without hiding a failed attempt. Only describe voice behavior that this recording proves. If speech fails on the recording device, keep the typed demo and state that voice remains unverified.

Before sharing the video, check that it plays while signed out, stays under three minutes, shows both apps, and matches the public [entry copy](./devpost-entry.md). The [official rules](https://kiroween.devpost.com/rules) bar third-party trademarks and copyrighted music in the video without permission. Keep branded items and music out of the recording. Do not reuse the deleted video link.
