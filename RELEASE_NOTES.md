# Mermail Policy Change Radar demo

This release contains a 4-minute, 21-second demo of the Policy Change Radar skill.

The recording shows:

- the public upstream skill pull request;
- the exact English prompt;
- a live Mermail inbox search;
- four synthetic policy notices being read;
- the completed, evidence-backed comparison;
- ranked effective dates and review questions; and
- the safety boundary against sending email or accepting terms.

The demo uses only clearly labeled synthetic policy notices.
